# Portfolio

The personal website of Valerio Farrotti, a freelance backend developer, showing prospective clients what Valerio has built and knows.

## Language

### People

**Client**:
An organization Valerio has done or does paid work for. Employers-of-record are Clients too, and so is an end customer reached through another Client's subcontract.
_Avoid_: Employer, customer, company

**Visitor**:
A prospective Client reading the site. The site is written for them first.
_Avoid_: Recruiter, user, reader

### Evidence

**Evidence**:
Commits authored by Valerio in a repository, the basis every Claim must stand on. The one exception is a Claim about career length or timeline, which the employment record (resume, LinkedIn) can back. Claims about skills always need commits.
_Avoid_: Proof, track record

**Claim**:
A statement on the site about Valerio's skills, experience or results (e.g. "6+ years", "Django"). A Claim is kept only if Evidence supports it.
_Avoid_: Statement, bullet

**Accomplishment**:
A specific piece of Shipped work worth highlighting, ranked by impact, then technical depth, then ownership. Commit volume only breaks ties.
_Avoid_: Achievement, highlight

### What the site shows

**Shipped work**:
Work delivered to a Client, running in production, or publicly released as an open-source product. The only kind of work that backs Claims about professional skill.
_Avoid_: Professional work, real work

**Case study**:
The site's write-up of one Client engagement (or one open-source product), led by its strongest Accomplishment with at most two supporting ones. It is anonymous unless the underlying repository is public.
_Avoid_: Project page, portfolio item

**Exploration**:
A study, hobby or experiment repository. It shows hands-on curiosity and never backs a Claim about professional skill.
_Avoid_: Side project, toy project, playground
